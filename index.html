About

I'm a 400-level Electrical & Electronics Engineering student at Michael Okpara University of Agriculture, Umudike. My degree teaches me how physical systems work — circuits, power, control. Alongside it, I'm deliberately building software and AI skills, because the most interesting problems I see sit at the point where both worlds meet.

I don't build projects for the sake of tutorials. StreetKash started from a real connectivity problem at a bus terminal. The Nollywood recommender came from wondering whether an AI could actually understand local film taste. The church website came from a real community that needed one. The answers aren't always polished, but they're grounded.

I build entirely from an Android phone — Acode, Termux, GitHub, Vercel. That constraint has shaped how I approach work: start with what functions, understand why it functions, then improve from there.

StreetKash

Sponsored public WiFi access for high-traffic locations in Abia State.

Reliable internet at busy public locations — bus terminals, markets — is expensive or absent. StreetKash explores a model where local businesses sponsor WiFi access points for their surrounding area, creating free connectivity in exchange for exposure.

Originally conceived as a reward-based streetlight advertising system. Current direction focuses on sponsored captive portal infrastructure. Top 10 — Abia 3MTT NextGen Knowledge Showcase.

Concept React Captive Portal Network Design

Nollywood Movie Recommender

Content-based film recommendation system built for Nollywood titles using AI/ML.

Standard recommendation datasets focus on Hollywood. This project explores whether a content-based filtering approach — using TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity — can surface relevant Nollywood films from a local dataset. Built as the 3MTT NextGen capstone project.

Python TF-IDF Cosine Similarity Pandas Google Colab

Redemption Parish — PCN

Official website for Presbyterian Church of Nigeria, Redemption Parish, Umuahia.

A church community needed a complete digital presence — sermon archive, event calendar, ministry pages, and a giving system. Designed and built as sole developer, deployed on Vercel.
